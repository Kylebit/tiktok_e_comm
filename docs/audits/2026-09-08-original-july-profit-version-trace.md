## Historical calculation and source contract

- Only official settled/released Finance records enter profit. Pending, processing, cancelled, and
  delivered-but-unsettled records are excluded.
- Each platform/site is independent. Only versioned canonical SKU costs and a live FX snapshot are
  shared.
- Weekly estimated advertising defaults to 22% of buyer cash-paid product amount, excluding buyer
  shipping; it is not 22% of net settlement.
- The Shopee monthly builder accepts an explicit operator-authorized estimated rate. The resulting
  calculation kind is `realized_settlement_with_estimated_ads`, never actual advertising.
- Cost reads use `shop.db` in SQLite read-only mode; the report retains cost and FX versions.
- Shopee fulfillment rules are site-specific: TH uses imported VAT plus duty, MY uses non-zero LVG
  sales tax, VN uses non-zero import VAT, and PH is cross-border.
- The expected historical artifact family was
  `artifacts/profit_reports_monthly/2026-07/shopee_{TH,MY,PH,VN}_2026-07-01_2026-07-31.monthly-profit.{json,html}`.
